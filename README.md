# 🌟 Inclusive Virtual Museum – For All Abilities

This is an **academic project** created using [Artsteps](https://www.artsteps.com) to design an **inclusive virtual museum** dedicated to people with **disabilities of all types** (visual, auditory, motor, cognitive, etc.). The experience combines **education, narration, and motivation** to inspire, inform, and raise awareness.

---

## 🎯 Project Goals

- Promote **empathy and understanding** of different disabilities.
- Provide an **educational and emotional journey** through storytelling and accessible content.
- **Motivate** people with disabilities by showcasing inspiring figures and stories.
- Encourage **equal opportunities** and **inclusive thinking** through a virtual 3D space.

## 🖼️ Museum Content Highlights

- **Biographies** of figures who overcame disabilities (e.g., *Helen Keller, Taha Hussein, Esref Armagan*).
- **Interactive artworks** like *The Mona Lisa*, *The Scream*, and *Van Gogh’s Portrait of Dr. Gachet*, adapted for accessibility.
- **Narrated stories** and **audio guides** for enhanced experience.
- A **fun sound-based guessing game** at the end to engage and reward the visitors.

---

## 🛠️ Technologies & Tools

- 🎨 **Artsteps** for virtual space creation
- 🔊 **Audio narration** for accessibility
- 🧠 **Storytelling techniques** for emotional connection
- 🧭 User flow adapted for keyboard/screen-reader navigation
